About Us

About Pearlscan Diagnostics

Pearlscan Diagnostics is a specialized diagnostic facility offering ultrasound, X-ray and laboratory services, including interventional procedures like HSG. We are built on clinical precision, modern equipment and rapid reporting.

We support general medicine, obstetrics, gynecology, fertility workups, painless HSG and other X-ray special procedures, using advanced ultrasound consoles with HD multi-frequency probes and color Doppler.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about booking, preparation and your results.

01 How do I book a scan?

Message us on WhatsApp (0803 263 4619) or send an email to pearlscandiagnostic@gmail.com and tell us which scan you need. We will guide you from there.

02 What should I bring with me?

Bring your doctor's request and any previous scan reports you have. If you are pregnant, bring your antenatal card too.

03 Do I need to prepare before my scan?

It depends on the scan. Some pelvic scans need a comfortably full bladder, and some abdominal, renal or X-ray studies need a few hours of fasting. Please confirm the exact preparation with us when you book.

04 Who reads my scan?

Your scan is performed by our trained sonographers and radiographers, and every report is interpreted and signed by an experienced consultant radiologist, so your result comes from a specialist.

05 How will I get my results?

You receive a thermal printout and a digital patient report package, delivered promptly after your scan.

06 Is an ultrasound scan safe?

Ultrasound is non-invasive and radiation-free. It uses sound waves to create the images, which is why it is widely used for pregnancy and women's health checks.

07 Is the HSG procedure painful?

We do a painless HSG using a gentle technique, performed by a consultant radiologist. Some people still feel mild cramping, which usually settles quickly.

08 What services do you offer?

Obstetric and gynecological ultrasound, general diagnostic ultrasound, vascular and Doppler studies, X-ray procedures such as HSG and barium studies, and clinical laboratory tests. See the full list on our services page.
